# Reposts & Likes — make.fun's newest X posts

An entry for the make.fun bounty "Get the most reposts and likes on
make.fun's newest X posts": posts of my own on X, each one a quote of, or
a direct reply to, a different one of make.fun's newest posts on
x.com/makefunai, every one carrying make.fun and one of my play codes in
its own words. `main.ts` hands the grader the posts' addresses by copying
`POSTS.json` to the output directory; the house reads each post's
reposts, quotes and likes itself, and the score is 2 points per repost
and per quote plus 1 per like, summed over all the posts. The draft text
of every post lives in `DRAFTS.md`.
